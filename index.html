Here is the updated version of your Bible Reading Tracker. I have integrated all your requested features while maintaining the sleek, mobile-first design and fixing the logic. Key Additions & Changes: Multiple Plans & No Progress Loss: The app now saves plans as individual profiles. You can switch freely between "One Year", "90 Day", "NT in 30 Days", and the new "Freestyle" plan without losing your checkmarks. Freestyle Plan: A totally open-ended mode. The dashboard acts as a log. You just go to the Reader, pick a chapter, start the timer, and when you save, it logs exactly what you read. Pace Setting (Pause Days / No Pressure): In Settings -> Reading, you can now toggle between "Strict Calendar" (shows missed days based on date) and "Flexible (No Pressure)". In Flexible mode, "Today" is simply the next uncompleted day in your list—you never fall behind. "Scrolls" Tab (TikTok/Reels Style): Added a highly immersive Scrolls tab next to the Read tab. It fetches verses and puts them into full-screen, snap-scrolling cards. You can "Like" (❤️) verses, leave "Comments" (💬), and it automatically remembers exactly which verse you left off on. Fun Stats: Removed all "Estimated Time" logic. Added fun, creative stats: Divine Inquiries: Times you've asked the AI a question. Night Owl: Readings completed past 10 PM. Scrolls Unrolled: Total chapters devoured. Verse Voyager: Number of unique books explored. Auto-Resume Reader: The Read tab now automatically saves your exact Book and Chapter.
